# Desk cable guide brief

Create an editable single-mesh desk cable guide with an open channel along its depth. The target outer dimensions are 50 mm wide, 24 mm deep, and 15 mm high. Use an 8 mm channel opening and put the bottom of the guide at ground level.

Save the editable source as `cable-guide.blend` and export only the guide as `cable-guide.glb`. In a clean Blender scene, reimport the GLB and verify the mesh count and triangle count. The three outer dimensions should match the evaluated source within 0.1 mm.

The example authorizes work on the disposable fixture files and a short project note. Keep publication, engine runtime, and visual approval outside this check.
